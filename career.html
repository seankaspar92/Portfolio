The decision, not just the document

The system treats a job posting as a decision problem. It extracts atomic requirements, maps them to verified evidence, identifies gaps and uncertainty, evaluates practical constraints, and produces an explainable recommendation before application effort is committed.

Governance principle: a high ATS score cannot turn a poor-fit role into an APPLY decision, and a strong-fit role does not justify inflating the resume to cross an ATS threshold.

FICTIONAL ROLE · PROGRAM OPERATIONS MANAGER

Mid-sized technology organization

Coordinate cross-functional programs, manage execution risk, improve operating processes, communicate with senior stakeholders, and use data to support decisions.

8.6 / 10illustrative governed Career Fit result

Source posting

The system begins with the complete role description rather than a title-only match. It preserves employer wording while normalizing repeated or overlapping requirements.

  • Lead cross-functional execution
  • Manage risk, priorities, and dependencies
  • Improve processes and operating rhythm
  • Communicate with senior stakeholders
  • Use data to support decisions

Practical constraints

Role quality is evaluated separately from capability fit.

  • Level: Manager
  • Work arrangement: compatible
  • Compensation: assumed in target band for demonstration
  • Start timing: compatible for demonstration
  • Hard technical license: none stated

Must-have requirements

  • Program / operations leadership
  • Cross-functional coordination
  • Execution ownership
  • Risk and issue management
  • Senior stakeholder communication

Preferred / differentiating

  • Technology environment experience
  • Analytics fluency
  • Process-improvement methods
  • Formal business / program education
  • Direct civilian industry exposure
Requirement strength is preserved. Preferred criteria do not silently become hard gates.

What the walkthrough demonstrates

Structured reasoning

The system decomposes a posting before scoring it, reducing title-only and keyword-only matching.

Evidence integrity

Direct, transferable, partial, and missing evidence remain distinct through the decision.

Decision governance

Fit, ATS, and practical constraints operate together without turning either metric into a hiring prediction.

Claims boundary

Not claimed: automated hire probability or guaranteed outcomes. The sample scores above are explicitly illustrative to demonstrate the governed workflow; production scoring requires complete authoritative inputs.
