What the learner must demonstrate

  1. Cite the controlling and observed records, with expected versus observed values.
  2. Prepare an actionable handoff that distinguishes proposed ownership and dates from recorded acceptance.
  3. Apply the exercise HOLD rule while preserving the Demo event lead's decision authority.
  4. Connect an AAR to a process correction and a revised learner task with an observable standard.

Proposed pass: at least 16 of 18 points, with full credit on all five critical criteria. The guide provides criterion-specific remediation and calls for reassessment with equivalent new records. The optional instructor extension adds 15 minutes outside the core lesson and grants no designation.

Follow the improvement into HOPS

The reference connects a late reconciliation issue to an AAR, a source-check correction and an independent comparison task. It ends at TRAINING_REVISED, with evaluation and closure pending. An evaluator must observe the criterion before recording a simulated result.
