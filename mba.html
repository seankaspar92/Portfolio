CENTERPIECE · MBA 598 PROGRAM CAPSTONE

New Horizons Airlines · Boardroom Decision Brief

A fictional airline case asking how to restore profitability while pursuing growth. The team recommendation prioritized fleet modernization first, followed by selective Caribbean expansion and later domestic scaling.

EXECUTIVE DECISION

Modernize First → Expand Selectively

Strengthen the operating foundation before committing to simultaneous expansion across multiple fronts.

CASE SIGNALS

Why change was required

  • Net margin fell from 3.98% to 2.97% in the supplied case data.
  • Load factor remained approximately 90%, indicating demand alone did not resolve profitability pressure.
  • The case fleet included 425 Boeing 737-800 aircraft alongside rising labor and fuel-cost pressure.
STRATEGIC CHOICE

What the team prioritized

  • Fleet modernization before broad expansion.
  • Caribbean routes as a controlled international growth test.
  • Selective domestic growth only after modernization progress.
FINANCIAL MODEL

How the decision was tested

  • Five-year valuation and discounted cash-flow analysis.
  • Base, best and worst-case scenarios.
  • 7.95% WACC and 3% terminal-growth assumptions in the team workbook.
GOVERNANCE

How execution was bounded

  • Phased rollout rather than one irreversible capital commitment.
  • Milestone and KPI gates across modernization and expansion.
  • Explicit execution, supplier, capital and regulatory/infrastructure risks.
PHASE 1Q1 2025 – Q4 202725% fleet modernization + Caribbean launch
PHASE 2Q1 2028 – Q4 203050% fleet modernization + selective domestic growth
PHASE 3Q1 2031 – Q4 2034Full modernization
Executive recommendation

Approve phased fleet modernization as the first strategic move, use Caribbean expansion as the controlled international test, and defer broader growth until operational and financial gates support it.
