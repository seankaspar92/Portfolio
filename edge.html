One methodology, different maturity

NFL EDGE has a connected application layer, validation controls, research isolation, historical provenance rules, and prospective decision-support architecture.

MLB EDGE is deliberately earlier in the lifecycle: ingestion, data quality, temporal integrity, reproducibility, and Baseball Operations foundations are being validated before downstream model claims.

The portfolio does not present the two systems as equally mature. Maturity is tied to executed evidence, not visual symmetry.

Verified NFL EDGE evidence

Connected decision dashboard

v2.7 exposes a local dashboard that refreshes structured snapshot data and populates Command Center KPIs, opportunity cards, Best Bets, activity, exposure, Model Registry, and Research Lab views.

Transport-only connector

The connector does not calculate picks, change signal state, resize stakes, or promote research models. Production output stays upstream of presentation.

Research firewall

Payloads with an explicitly unlocked research firewall fail validation. Research remains in a separate namespace and cannot create production opportunities through the connector.

Fail-visible fallback

Missing, malformed, invalid, or unreachable live data switches the interface to an explicit DEMO FALLBACK state rather than silently presenting demo data as live.

Feature provenance

Historical features must satisfy the temporal invariant available_at <= prediction_lock_at, with immutable manifest and feature-level provenance records.

Authentic historical prices

Historical evaluation does not invent synthetic -110 odds. Unpriced observations can support football-result diagnostics but are excluded from profit/ROI gates.

Historical replay boundary: chronological/rolling-origin controls, prediction-lock timing, provenance, authentic-price coverage rules, deterministic identity, and idempotent persistence are part of the validated foundation. Model-performance claims remain experiment-specific and require their own evidence.

MLB EDGE evidence boundary

MLB EDGE remains an active local build. The current portfolio claim is intentionally limited to ingestion, validation, data architecture, research methodology, and Baseball Operations analytical design.

It is not presented as a released production forecasting platform, and no model-performance statistic is promoted simply because a research run exists.

Next maturity gate: close the known validator defect, rerun the authentic Statcast fixture, then advance through immutable raw archive, schema fingerprinting, identity/history, bronze/silver layers, point-in-time features, baseline projections, rolling-origin validation, uncertainty, and calibration before expanding model claims.

What EDGE demonstrates

Data engineering

Source ingestion, schema discipline, provenance, quarantine, identity, temporal integrity, and reproducible snapshots.

Model governance

Research isolation, prospective eligibility, promotion gates, authentic evaluation inputs, and explicit limitations.

Decision science

Turning validated analytical outputs into explainable operational decision support without letting the presentation layer manufacture conclusions.
