01 · RESOURCE STEWARDSHIP

Multimillion-Dollar Resource Accountability

OPERATING PROBLEM
Maintain readiness and accountability for high-value equipment while simultaneously meeting training and operational requirements.
SCOPE
Weapons Squad Leader duty descriptions included more than $4M in assigned equipment; a later rating period documented more than $3M in equipment readiness and accountability. Instructor responsibilities later included more than $500K in weapons systems and associated items.
RESULT
Maintained strong accountability and operational readiness across assignments without blending separate time-period values.
CIVILIAN CAPABILITY
Asset management, internal controls, operational readiness, compliance, resource stewardship.
Sources: NCOER Nov 2022–Nov 2023; NCOER Nov 2023–Jul 2024; NCOER Jul 2024–Jul 2025.
02 · TRAINING PROGRAM EXECUTION

Large-Scale Training at Scale

OPERATING PROBLEM
Deliver high-risk technical training at large scale while preserving standardization, safety, and throughput.
SCOPE
Executed 15 Heavy Machine Gun live-fire ranges, trained approximately 4,800 Soldiers during live-fire operations, conducted PMI for approximately 600 Soldiers, and supported annual instruction and mentorship for approximately 12,000 Soldiers.
RESULT
Met training requirements while maintaining zero accidents or injuries during the documented Heavy Machine Gun ranges.
CIVILIAN CAPABILITY
Training program management, technical instruction, workforce development, safety, standardization.
Source: NCOER Jul 2024–Jul 2025.
03 · WORKFORCE DEVELOPMENT

Drill Sergeant Leadership

OPERATING PROBLEM
Transform large cohorts of new personnel into disciplined, standards-based teams on a repeatable training cycle.
SCOPE
Responsible for training, coaching, and mentoring more than 1,600 civilians into Soldiers during Drill Sergeant and Senior Drill Sergeant service.
RESULT
Selected as Drill Sergeant of the Cycle for 10 May 2021 through 15 July 2021, demonstrating recognized leadership and training performance.
CIVILIAN CAPABILITY
Onboarding, talent development, coaching, performance management, instructional leadership.

High-Risk Operations and Safety Control

OPERATING PROBLEM

SCOPE
Fifteen Heavy Machine Gun live-fire ranges with thousands of trainees and repeated hazard identification and mitigation responsibilities.
RESULT
Zero accidents or injuries across the documented Heavy Machine Gun range sequence.
CIVILIAN CAPABILITY

Source: NCOER Jul 2024–Jul 2025.
05 · PERFORMANCE IMPROVEMENT

Measurement, Coaching, and Readiness

OPERATING PROBLEM
Improve team readiness through measurable standards, structured coaching, and consistent follow-through.
SCOPE
Raised squad ACFT average from 509 to 526 during one rating period; a later program produced a 537 squad average. Facilitated three company ACFTs with a 98% pass rate and 100% of the company having an ACFT on record.
RESULT
Demonstrated repeatable performance improvement through tracked standards and deliberate development.
CIVILIAN CAPABILITY
KPI improvement, coaching, workforce performance, process improvement, performance management.
Sources: NCOER Nov 2022–Nov 2023; NCOER Nov 2023–Jul 2024.
06 · COMPLEX OPERATIONS

Coordination Across Competing Requirements

OPERATING PROBLEM
Coordinate people, equipment, logistics, and stakeholders across simultaneous operational requirements.
SCOPE
Supported NTC rail load-out operations for more than 2,200 vehicles; separately supported movement operations involving more than 900 trainees; during Cobra Gold coordinated training across 30 Royal Thai Army and 12 U.S. gun teams.
RESULT
Maintained execution visibility and coordination across logistics, training, and multinational stakeholders.
CIVILIAN CAPABILITY
Program execution, logistics, stakeholder coordination, operations management, cross-cultural collaboration.
Sources: NCOER Nov 2023–Jul 2024; PCS AAM Jun 2024; leadership/interview evidence for trainee movement.
