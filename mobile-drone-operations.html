The problem and my role

Launching a system is only one part of field use. Teams also need recovery options, clear decision authority, support ownership, and a way to turn observations into better training.

I designed and packaged the mission thread, clickable concept demonstrations, and proposed handoffs between readiness, recovery, and training. The concept does not establish physical recovery performance.

One mission, four connected views

Synthetic concept storyboard

  3. Decide

  4. Improve

    The AAR records the condition, decision, unresolved issue, and training follow-up.
