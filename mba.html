CENTERPIECE · MBA 598 PROGRAM CAPSTONE

New Horizons Airlines

The capstone tested whether a fictional airline facing margin compression should pursue international growth immediately or modernize its operating foundation first. The final recommendation was a phased modernization strategy followed by selective expansion.

STRATEGIC MODERNIZATION PLAN

Modernize first. Expand selectively.

The team framed declining profitability as an operating-model problem rather than a demand problem, then evaluated fleet, digital, network, cost structure, expansion, implementation, and risk as one integrated decision.

1 · DEFINEDeclining profitability and growth pressure
2 · DIAGNOSEFleet, digital, network and cost drivers
3 · COMPAREStatus quo vs. expand now vs. modernize first
4 · VALIDATEMargins, fleet economics and market data
5 · SEQUENCEPhased roadmap with milestone gates

Financial validation

Case figures showed revenue remaining broadly stable while EBIT and net income declined from 2017 to 2019. The analysis treated this as evidence that demand alone was not solving the profitability problem.

Fleet strategy

Use phased domestic fleet renewal as the baseline, preserve capital flexibility, and delay widebody commitments until international route economics and infrastructure are proven.

Digital modernization

Modernize web and mobile booking to reduce customer friction, improve data capture, and strengthen the service proposition for business and leisure travelers.

Network strategy

Protect the domestic core, use Caribbean expansion as a controlled first international test, and defer Northern Europe until fleet, hub, and demand readiness improve.

Implementation

Sequence foundation work, core modernization, international testing, and selective scaling across a multi-phase roadmap rather than making one large irreversible bet.

Risk & governance

Address fleet capital burden, technology delays, international demand uncertainty, competitive response, and execution complexity with staged rollout, executive ownership, and milestone reviews.

Board-level recommendation

Approve a phased strategic modernization program, restore operating strength first, and use selective Caribbean expansion as the initial international test rather than pursuing immediate long-haul growth.
