# Mobile Drone Operations concept evidence

Reviewed October 17, 2026.

## Existing concept artifacts

Published concept text was inspected for Mobile Drone Operations System v1.1 and Drone Recovery v1.1 Mission Thread. The first frames readiness, mobile launch, degraded recovery, operator approval, AAR, and training adoption. The recovery demonstration progresses through setup, launch, degraded-condition injection, node comparison, operator approval, recovery, and AAR handoff.

## What the public page demonstrates

The public page explains the operating sequence and shows a newly authored synthetic decision-record example. It is a concept storyboard, not a live execution of either hosted demo.

## What remains unproven

Physical launch/recovery performance; cue or beacon performance; mobile localization; flight control; validated navigation, battery, and safety thresholds; connected telemetry; shared backend handoffs; simulation and field acceptance.

Training and engineering follow-ups are proposed integration paths. No employer sponsorship, approved product integration, service endorsement, or operational deployment is claimed. Existing hosted-demo access remains restricted.
