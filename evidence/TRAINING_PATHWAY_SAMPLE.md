# Proposed training pathway and assessment sample

Public extract prepared October 17, 2026 from the September 17, 2026 Version 2 development draft. Independent concept; no company sponsorship or service approval is claimed.

## Placement

| Pathway | Proposed instruction | Entry profile |
|---|---|---|
| Four weeks | 20 days / 120 hours | New users selected for instructor development |
| Two weeks | 10 days / 60 hours | Experienced personnel with verified foundations |
| One week | 5 days / 30 hours | Current product/version proficiency with entry practical evidence |

All hours, resources, standards, and intervals require technical and customer validation. Missing critical-task evidence cannot be waived by choosing a shorter course.

## Separate progression gates

Product gate: applicable references, roles and limits, records, and individual approved product tasks.

Instructor gate: objective-aligned lesson plan, observed teaching, consistent assessment/remediation, and controlled administrative records. Current product proficiency is required. Attendance alone satisfies neither gate.
