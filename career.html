The decision, not just the document

The system treats a job posting as a decision problem. It extracts atomic requirements, maps them to verified evidence, identifies gaps and uncertainty, evaluates practical constraints, and produces an explainable recommendation before application effort is committed.

Governance principle: a high ATS score cannot turn a poor-fit role into an APPLY decision, and a strong-fit role does not justify inflating the resume to cross an ATS threshold.

Five integrated intelligence layers

1 · Requirement extraction

Structures responsibilities, qualifications, preferred criteria, seniority, compensation, geography, timing, and practical constraints.

2 · Evidence matching

Maps requirements to authoritative resume, CV, project, education, certification, and career-history evidence.

3 · Governed fit-gap analysis

Uses the Career Fit model to determine whether the opportunity deserves application effort while preserving genuine gaps.

4 · ATS + application strategy

Tailors application materials against verified evidence and measures truthful ATS alignment rather than optimizing for keyword volume.

5 · Opportunity intelligence

Reconciles employers, business units, requisitions, applications, recruiter context, duplicates, status changes, and follow-up history.

Explainability throughout

Material decisions retain their evidence, assumptions, uncertainty, gaps, and reasons so recommendations can be challenged and improved.

Why the recommendation is explainable

  • Core capabilities are supported by evidence.
  • Preferred qualifications are separated from hard requirements.
  • Known gaps and unknowns remain visible.
  • Practical constraints are evaluated separately from skill fit.
  • Application materials cannot introduce unsupported claims.

Claims boundary

Not claimed: automated hire probability or guaranteed outcomes. Fit and ATS metrics are governed decision-support measures, not predictions of an employer's hiring decision.
