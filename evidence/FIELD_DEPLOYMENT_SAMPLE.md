# Proposed field deployment checklist and pilot scorecard

Public working example prepared October 17, 2026. Independent framework; no company engagement or completed pilot is claimed.

## Readiness review

| Review area | Required evidence | Accountable role |
|---|---|---|
| Objective | Purpose, boundaries, success criteria, authority | Event lead/customer representative |
| System | Product/configuration identity, references, limitations | Technical reviewer |
| People/site | Prerequisites, resources, access, approved site requirements | Event lead |
| Support | Issue route, evidence handoff, update cadence, closeout | Support owner |

Record unresolved evidence as open. An accepting authority must decide how a blocker affects the event.

## Issue handoff record

Issue identifier; observed condition; configuration; reproducible evidence; severity; owner; next-update time; disposition; verification evidence; customer communication owner. A sent report is not a resolved issue.

## Proposed pilot scorecard

- Readiness evidence completeness: satisfied applicable items / applicable required items, with critical blockers reported separately.
- Operator proficiency: assessed tasks with evidence / required tasks, by operator. Do not substitute attendance for task proficiency.
- Issue handling: acknowledgment and verified closure intervals, with unresolved issues visible.
- AAR follow-through: actions closed with verification / actions due, with owners and dates.

No target, improvement percentage, or customer impact is claimed. Define baselines, scope, denominators, exclusions, and acceptance criteria with the pilot owner before measurement.
