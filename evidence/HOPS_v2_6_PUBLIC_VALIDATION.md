Date: 2026-09-12
Status: Release Candidate
Scope: Independent synthetic portfolio prototype

## Evidence boundary

HOPS is an independently developed, synthetic operations planning and decision-support prototype. This evidence does not represent an employer or third-party product, internal system, operational deployment, government approval, production authorization, or third-party adoption.

All v2.6 demonstration records are synthetic. They do not represent actual Havoc or partner production capacity, workforce, suppliers, inventory, configuration approvals, acceptance queues, or operational status.

## v2.6 scope

v2.6 adds a distributed industrial-readiness lane above the previously demonstrated partner-fleet sustainment and integration-qualification lanes. The synthetic lane represents:

- platform/autonomy configuration identity
- non-binding monthly planning capacity
- qualified-workforce coverage
- critical-spares depth
- supplier constraints
- acceptance queues
- reconstitution readiness

The release-candidate demo state contains four synthetic industrial nodes, a non-binding aggregate planning capacity of 36 units per month, three of four verified synthetic platform/autonomy pairings, 15 synthetic assets in an acceptance queue, and four review findings. These values exist only to exercise the prototype workflow.

## Human-authority boundary

HOPS may aggregate, compare, flag, forecast, and package evidence for human review. It does not have authority to:

- approve a configuration
- release production
- accept an asset
- order spares
- task a workforce
- obligate funds
- make legal or acquisition determinations
- issue mission tasking

Human authorities remain controlling. Unresolved conditions remain visible as review items rather than being converted into automatic approval.
