Job Posting→Requirements→Evidence Match
Fit + Gaps→Constraints + Uncertainty→Pursue / Conditional / Pass

No fabricated match percentage or hiring-probability score is required for the recommendation.

The decision, not just the document

The system treats a job posting as a decision problem. It extracts requirements, distinguishes must-have from preferred criteria, maps them to verified evidence, identifies gaps and uncertainty, evaluates practical constraints, and produces an explainable pursue / conditional / pass recommendation.

The graphic depicts the intended end-to-end operating concept. Live job aggregation and generated automation percentages are not claimed unless separately implemented and measured.

Verified workflow

Requirements extraction

Structures qualifications, responsibilities, preferred criteria, and practical constraints.

Evidence matching

Maps requirements to verified resume, CV, and career-history evidence.

Gap analysis

Surfaces missing, ambiguous, or unsupported criteria instead of silently assuming fit.

Practical fit

Considers location, availability, transition timing, and other role constraints when relevant.

Decision support

Produces an explainable pursue / conditional / pass recommendation rather than a black-box score.

Application strategy

Uses the evidence map to guide targeted resume, interview, and outreach emphasis.

SANITIZED DECISION CASE

From job posting to explainable pursue decision.

This fictionalized example demonstrates the Career Intelligence workflow without representing an actual employer decision or application outcome.

FICTIONAL ROLE INPUT DEMONSTRATION

Program Operations Manager

Mid-sized technology organization seeking an operations leader to coordinate cross-functional programs, manage execution risk, improve processes, communicate with senior stakeholders, and use data to support decisions.

Cross-functional leadership Program execution Process improvement Data-driven decisions Executive communication

Step 1 · Requirement extraction

Must-have

People/program leadership, cross-functional coordination, execution ownership, stakeholder communication, and process improvement.

Preferred

Technology environment experience, analytics fluency, formal project/program credentials, and direct civilian industry exposure.

Practical constraints

Location, start-date compatibility, compensation range, travel requirements, and any role-specific schedule expectations.

Step 2 · Evidence map

Requirement Evidence status Supporting evidence Interpretation
Lead teams / programs STRONG Documented leadership of multi-person teams and complex operational work Directly transferable
Cross-functional coordination STRONG Coordination across stakeholders, functions, deadlines, resources, and dependencies Directly transferable
Process improvement SUPPORTED Evidence of standardization, execution improvement, and operating discipline Translate into business terminology
Data-driven decision support SUPPORTED Analytics projects, dashboards, evidence-based prioritization, and decision systems Strong supporting differentiator
Direct civilian technology operations GAP / PARTIAL AI/software portfolio and formal education, but limited direct industry tenure Do not conceal; mitigate with project evidence

Step 3 · Fit, gaps, and uncertainty

Strong alignment

Leadership, operational execution, prioritization, stakeholder coordination, risk management, and process discipline.

Transferable differentiators

AI Operating System, analytics platforms, MBA-level business grounding, and structured AI implementation work strengthen the civilian translation.

Real gaps

Limited direct tenure inside a civilian technology company and possible tool/domain differences. These remain explicit rather than being scored away.

Uncertainty rule: missing information is marked unknown. The system does not convert missing evidence into assumed qualification.

Step 4 · Recommendation

DECISION

PURSUE

The core work aligns strongly with verified leadership and operations evidence. The primary gap is direct civilian-industry tenure, but the portfolio provides credible technical and AI implementation evidence that supports a transition case.

Why this is explainable

  • Core required capabilities are supported by evidence.
  • Preferred qualifications are separated from hard requirements.
  • Known gaps remain visible.
  • Practical constraints are checked separately from skill fit.
  • The decision does not depend on a fabricated “87% match” score.

Step 5 · Application strategy

Resume emphasis

Lead with people leadership, program execution, operational scale, process improvement, and measurable accountability. Use AI/analytics projects as differentiating evidence.

Interview emphasis

Prepare stories around competing priorities, unexpected operational challenges, influencing stakeholders, improving execution, and learning new technical domains.

Gap mitigation

Address civilian-industry transition directly and point to shipped technical projects, formal business education, and applied AI work as evidence of translation speed.
