My role and the proposed pathways

I designed the course architecture, progression gates, schedules, instructor-development structure, and record requirements. The September 17 development draft separates product proficiency from the ability to teach and assess others.

Two separate proficiency decisions

Product proficiency

Reference selection, limits and escalation, records, and individual approved product tasks must have evidence before instructor assessment.

Instructor proficiency

Lesson planning, observed teaching, consistent learner assessment, remediation, and controlled course records require separate evidence. Attendance alone satisfies neither gate.
